# Content Notice: Third-Party Images in Scenes

## Scope

Some images in the `scenes/` section may be sourced from the internet and
modified to work with the xwww scene engine. Modifications may include
cropping, recoloring, compositing, palette adaptation, and the addition of
animation or other functional layers.

This notice applies to those third-party images and to any scene asset whose
provenance cannot be confirmed. It does not apply to original artwork, code,
or documentation created by xscriptor and the equisdots contributors.

## Licensing

Third-party images covered by this notice are excluded from the licensing
terms of this repository, including the CC BY 4.0 license used for original
artwork and documentation and the MIT license used for source code. No
ownership is claimed over those images, and no license is granted for their
reuse.

This is a non-commercial project. The images are included only as functional
parts of the scene collection and are not sold, licensed, or offered as
standalone assets.

## Removal requests

If you are the original owner of an image included in a scene and believe it
does not belong in this project, you can request its removal. Please contact
the maintainer through any support or contact channel listed on
[xscriptor's GitHub profile](https://github.com/xscriptor), including the
repository issue tracker.

A useful request includes:

- the scene name or directory;
- the image file or asset path;
- a link or other evidence showing that you are the original owner; and
- whether you want the image removed or replaced.

Valid removal requests will be reviewed and honored promptly. The scene may
be removed, the image may be replaced, or the asset may be adjusted as
appropriate. No commercial activity is being carried out with the material.
